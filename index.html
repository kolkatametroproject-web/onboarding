About MRPSP

The Metropolitan Rapid Public Service Program is an initiative to recruit talented individuals for key positions. This comprehensive onboarding platform streamlines the entire recruitment process from registration through final selection.

Key Stages:

  • Registration: Candidates register with basic information
  • Admit Card: Automated generation and download
  • Examination: Online exam with proctoring
  • Results: Instant evaluation and score publication
  • Interview: Scheduled interviews for selected candidates
  • Final Selection: Merit-based final selection
